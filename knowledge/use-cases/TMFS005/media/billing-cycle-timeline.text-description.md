# billing-cycle-timeline.png

**Type:** Timeline/Gantt-style illustration diagram.
**Source context:** `# Description` section, directly illustrating the
worked example of how successive monthly bills accumulate different
charge types over the product's lifecycle.

A horizontal timeline from 1st of September to 1st of January, marked
with the delivery date (13th of September), the delivery date of a new
option (25th of October), and the contract termination date (20th of
November). For each bill cycle it shows colored arrows spanning the
charge-collection period for that bill:

- **September Bill Cycle** — crossed out (no bill produced for this
  partial period).
- **October Bill Cycle** — orange arrows for Recurring Fees, Usage Fees,
  and One Time Fees spanning September 13th–30th, plus the same three
  fee types spanning October (recurring fees shown in orange, usage/one
  time fees transitioning to green as they roll into the November
  cycle).
- **November Bill Cycle** — green "All Recurring Fees" arrow, a short
  green "Option Recurring Fees" arrow starting October 25th, and
  green/blue Usage Fees and One Time Fees arrows, plus a blue "Recurring
  Fees Refund" arrow starting at the November 20th termination date.
- **December Bill Cycle** — labeled but not detailed further.
